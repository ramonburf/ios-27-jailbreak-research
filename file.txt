● Yes — --sockuaf with all three variants is already in v123, and the local copy
  matches the Mac byte-for-byte (1d347b2f…). So the port isn't the gap. The gap
  is that the ported race mechanics can't win: it spawns fresh threads per
  iteration, then has each thread poll with usleep(1) — so the close and the two
  send()s land milliseconds apart with essentially zero kernel overlap. And
  ~2.7 new 512 KB-stack threads per iteration is also the likely SIGKILL cause.

  I'll rewrite the race to use persistent spinning workers (created once), long
  in-kernel sends (8 KB into a 4 KB sndbuf), and a persistent closer.
